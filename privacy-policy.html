Rentora Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 September 2026

In short: Rentora works fully offline. Everything you enter is stored only on your device. We do not collect, receive, sell or share any of your data, and the app has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

Who we are

Rentora (“the app”) is a rental property manager for tracking properties, tenants, rent, expenses and invoices. This policy explains how the app handles information. “We” and “us” mean the developer of Rentora.

Information stored on your device

To do its job, the app stores the information you choose to enter. This can include:

  • Property details, such as names, addresses, rent amounts and notes.
  • Tenant details, such as names, phone numbers, email addresses, ID numbers, lease dates, rent, deposits and notes.
  • Income and expense records, invoices and payment information you record.
  • Photos and documents you attach to transactions, such as receipts and bills.
  • Your app settings, including any business details you add for invoices.

This information is saved in a database inside the app's private storage on your device. It is never sent to us or to any server we control. We cannot see, access or recover it.

Information we collect

None. Rentora does not collect personal information, usage data, device identifiers, location or crash reports. It contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software.

Device permissions

  • Camera: used only when you choose to take a photo of a receipt or bill to attach to a transaction.
  • Photos and files: used only when you choose to attach an existing photo or document, or to restore a backup. The app uses the system picker, so it can only see the items you select.
  • Phone dialer: tapping a tenant's phone number opens your phone's dialer. The app does not place calls itself.

Selected photos and documents are copied into the app's private storage and stay on your device.

Backups, invoices and reports you share

The app only creates files that leave its storage when you ask it to:

  • Backups: when you create a backup, the app saves a copy of your data (including attachments) to a folder you choose, or passes it to an app you choose from the share sheet.
  • PDF invoices and reports: when you export or share a PDF, it goes only to the destination you choose.

Once you save or send a file to another app or service, such as email, messaging or cloud storage, that service's own privacy policy applies. Backup files contain all of your Rentora data, so store them somewhere you trust.

Android system backup

If Android's built-in backup is turned on in your device settings, Android may include Rentora's data in your device backup to your Google account. This is controlled by Android and your Google account settings, not by us, and the data is not accessible to us.

Information about other people

Rentora lets you record details about your tenants. You are responsible for having a lawful basis to store their information and for handling it in line with the privacy laws that apply to you.

Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual records in the app at any time. Uninstalling Rentora or clearing its app data permanently removes all data stored by the app, except for any backup files you saved elsewhere.

Security

Your data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read under Android's normal security model. Keep your device protected with a screen lock and keep any backup files in a secure place.

Children's privacy

Rentora is intended for adults who manage rental properties. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
